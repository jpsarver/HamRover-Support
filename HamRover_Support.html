Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. HamRover has no accounts and sends nothing to a server. Your events, stops, targets, and contacts are included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud or computer).

Which contests are supported?

The ARRL 10 GHz and Up contest is fully supported. Use a Contest ID in the form ARRL-10-GHZ-YYYY, for example ARRL-10-GHZ-2026. Other grid-based VHF/UHF contests can be logged and exported with generic rules. To request full support for another contest, email me with its name.

How do I export my log?

On the Events tab, tap the export button on an event. HamRover reviews the log first, then lets you save or send the Cabrillo file. Events that share a Contest ID, such as both weekends of the same contest, are exported together in one file.

Why isn't a contact in my export?

Only contacts marked Completed are exported. Contacts marked Planned, Failed, or Skipped are left out, as are contacts you switched off on the review screen. The review screen lists how many were left out and why.

Why does the review screen flag duplicates?

A station can be worked once per band from each location. If the same station appears twice on the same band at the same stop, the review screen shows both so you can choose which one counts.
